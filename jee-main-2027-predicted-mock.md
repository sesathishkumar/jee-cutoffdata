# JEE Main 2027 · Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech.)

**Predicted practice shift · 180 minutes · 300 marks**

This original mock uses NTA’s 2027 syllabus and exam format. The topic mix reflects recurring question types from 2024–2026. Chapter counts can vary by shift.

Each subject has 20 single-correct MCQs and five numerical questions. For both sections, score **+4** for a correct response, **−1** for an incorrect response and **0** for an unanswered question. Round numerical answers to the nearest integer where needed.

## Mathematics

### Section A · Single-correct MCQs

1. If f(x) = (x−1)/(x+1), how many real x satisfy f(f(x)) = f(x), with both sides defined?  
   A. 0 · B. 1 · C. 2 · D. 3
2. The system x+y+z=1, 2x+3y+4z=2, x+2y+kz=1 has infinitely many solutions for which k?  
   A. 1 · B. 2 · C. 3 · D. 4
3. The roots of x²−6x+k=0 differ by 2. What is the sum of the reciprocals of the roots?  
   A. 1/2 · B. 3/4 · C. 2/3 · D. 4/3
4. An arithmetic progression has 4th term 11 and 10th term 29. What is the sum of the terms from the 5th to the 12th, inclusive?  
   A. 184 · B. 192 · C. 212 · D. 196
5. Find the constant term in (x²+2/x)⁹.  
   A. 5376 · B. 2688 · C. 1344 · D. 672
6. How many five-digit even numbers can be formed from 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 with no repetition?  
   A. 288 · B. 300 · C. 312 · D. 360
7. Box A contains 2 red and 1 blue ball. Box B contains 1 red and 2 blue balls. A box is selected uniformly and two balls are drawn from it without replacement. Given exactly one red ball is drawn, what is the probability that Box A was selected?  
   A. 1/3 · B. 2/3 · C. 3/4 · D. 1/2
8. What is the area enclosed by y=|x−1| and y=x²−2x+1?  
   A. 1/6 · B. 1/3 · C. 2/3 · D. 1
9. Evaluate lim(x→0) [ln(1+2x)−2ln(1+x)]/x².  
   A. 0 · B. 1 · C. −1 · D. −2
10. What is the absolute maximum of x³−3x² on [−1,3]?  
    A. 0 · B. 1 · C. 2 · D. 4
11. Evaluate ∫₀¹ x·ln[(1+x)/(2−x)] dx.  
    A. (3/2)ln 2 − 1 · B. 2ln 2 − 1 · C. 1 − ln 2 · D. 3/2 − 2ln 2
12. The function y satisfies x·dy/dx + 2y = x² for x>0, and y(1)=1. Find y(2).  
    A. 5/4 · B. 7/4 · C. 13/16 · D. 19/16
13. The common chord of x²+y²=25 and x²+y²−6x−8y=0 has length  
    A. 5 · B. 5√3 · C. 4√5 · D. 10.
14. The line x−y=0 cuts the parabola y²=8x at two points. What is the length of the chord?  
    A. 4 · B. 4√2 · C. 8√2 · D. 8
15. An ellipse with foci (±3,0) passes through (0,4). Find the length of its latus rectum.  
    A. 8/5 · B. 5 · C. 16/5 · D. 32/5
16. For a=(1,2,−1), b=(2,−1,1) and c=(1,0,2), find |a·(b×c)|.  
    A. 11 · B. 7 · C. 9 · D. 13
17. Find the shortest distance between the lines r=(1,0,0)+s(1,1,0) and r=(0,0,2)+t(1,−1,0).  
    A. 1 · B. √2 · C. 2 · D. √5
18. If |z−2|=|z+2| and |z−i|=2, what is the sum of the imaginary parts of all possible z?  
    A. −2 · B. 0 · C. 1 · D. 2
19. The mean and variance of 10 observations are 4 and 9, respectively, using divisor 10. If one observation of value 9 is replaced by 19, what is the new variance?  
    A. 28 · B. 29 · C. 30 · D. 31
20. How many solutions does sin(2x)=cos x have on [0,2π)?  
    A. 2 · B. 3 · C. 4 · D. 5

### Section B · Numerical answers

21. Find the number of onto functions from a five-element set to a three-element set.
22. Let A be the 2×2 matrix with rows (2,1) and (1,2). Find the trace of A⁴.
23. Balls are drawn without replacement from a bag containing three red and three blue balls. If the probability that the second red ball appears on the fourth draw is p/q in lowest terms, find p+q.
24. The line through (1,2,3) and (3,1,5) intersects the line through (5,0,7) with direction (1,1,1). Find the sum of the coordinates of their intersection.
25. Evaluate 12∫₀² |x²−3x+2| dx.

## Physics

Use g=10 m/s² wherever needed. Assume ideal components unless specified.

### Section A · Single-correct MCQs

26. Two charges +q are at x=−a and x=a. A third charge +Q of mass m is constrained to move along the x-axis near the origin. For small x, the force on it is approximately  
    A. −2kqQx/a³ · B. +2kqQx/a³ · C. −4kqQx/a³ · D. +4kqQx/a³.
27. A capacitor is charged and disconnected. A dielectric of relative permittivity 3 fills half the plate area and the entire gap in that half. Its final stored energy divided by its initial stored energy is  
    A. 1/3 · B. 1/2 · C. 2/3 · D. 2.
28. A 12 V source feeds a 4 Ω resistor in series with a parallel combination of 6 Ω and 3 Ω. What power is dissipated in the 3 Ω resistor?  
    A. 16/3 W · B. 8/3 W · C. 4 W · D. 12 W
29. The voltage across a cell is 5.0 V in open circuit and 4.0 V when connected across 8 Ω. Find its internal resistance.  
    A. 1 Ω · B. 4 Ω · C. 8 Ω · D. 2 Ω
30. A particle enters a uniform magnetic field at 60° to the field. Find the ratio of helix pitch to radius.  
    A. √3π · B. 2π/√3 · C. π/√3 · D. 2√3π
31. A 50-turn coil of area 0.02 m² has total resistance 2 Ω. A uniform field normal to it reverses from +0.4 T to −0.4 T. How much charge flows through the coil during the reversal?  
    A. 0.4 C · B. 0.2 C · C. 0.8 C · D. 1.6 C
32. At resonance, a series RLC circuit draws 2 A rms from a 100 V rms source. The inductor voltage is 160 V rms. Find the ratio of inductive reactance to resistance.  
    A. 0.625 · B. 0.8 · C. 1 · D. 1.6
33. A convex lens of focal length 20 cm is in contact with a concave lens of focal length −30 cm. An object is 90 cm in front of the combination. The image distance and magnification are  
    A. 180 cm, +2 · B. 180 cm, −2 · C. 36 cm, −0.4 · D. 60 cm, −2/3.
34. In a Young’s double-slit experiment, a film of thickness 3.0 μm and refractive index 1.5 covers one slit. The wavelength is 600 nm. The central fringe shifts by how many fringe spacings?  
    A. 1.5 · B. 2 · C. 2.5 · D. 5
35. A metal gives stopping potentials of 1 V and 3 V at incident frequencies f and 2f, respectively. What is its work function?  
    A. 0.5 eV · B. 2 eV · C. 3 eV · D. 1 eV
36. A hydrogen atom makes a transition from n=4 to n=2. The wavelength emitted is what fraction of the wavelength for the n=3 to n=2 transition?  
    A. 20/27 · B. 27/20 · C. 5/9 · D. 9/5
37. A nucleus of mass number 240 and binding energy per nucleon 7.6 MeV splits into two nuclei of mass number 120, each with binding energy per nucleon 8.4 MeV. How much energy is released?  
    A. 96 MeV · B. 192 MeV · C. 240 MeV · D. 384 MeV
38. An AND gate receives inputs A and B. A NAND gate then receives A and the output of that AND gate. What is the final output?  
    A. A · B. A OR B · C. NOT (A AND B) · D. NOT B
39. A uniform solid cylinder rolls without slipping from rest down a slope, descending a vertical height h. At the bottom its speed is  
    A. √(gh) · B. √(2gh) · C. √(4gh/3) · D. √(3gh/2).
40. A 2 kg block on a horizontal surface is pulled by a 20 N force inclined at 30° above the horizontal. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.2 and the block slides in the direction of the horizontal force component. What is its acceleration?  
    A. (5√3−1) m/s² · B. (5√3−2) m/s² · C. (10√3−1) m/s² · D. (5√3+1) m/s²
41. A satellite is in a circular orbit of radius r. What is the minimum additional tangential speed, as a fraction of its initial speed, required to escape?  
    A. 1/2 · B. 1/√2 · C. 1 · D. √2−1
42. Water of density 1000 kg/m³ flows steadily through a horizontal pipe with negligible viscosity. Its speed is 2 m/s where the area is A and the area then narrows to A/2. The pressure drop is  
    A. 2 kPa · B. 4 kPa · C. 6 kPa · D. 8 kPa.
43. One mole of a monatomic ideal gas expands along the path P=aV from volume V₀ to 2V₀. If the work done by the gas is W, the heat absorbed is  
    A. 2W · B. 3W · C. 4W · D. 5W.
44. An ideal spring-mass oscillator passes through x=A/2, where A is the amplitude. Its kinetic energy divided by its potential energy is  
    A. 3 · B. 1/3 · C. 1/2 · D. 2.
45. A quantity is computed as X=ab²/c³. If the maximum percentage uncertainties in a, b and c are 1%, 2% and 1%, respectively, what is the first-order maximum percentage uncertainty in X?  
    A. 4% · B. 8% · C. 6% · D. 10%

### Section B · Numerical answers

46. A 3 μF capacitor charged to 12 V is connected in parallel, like plates together, to a 9 μF capacitor charged to 4 V. Find the energy lost in μJ.
47. A 10 V battery of internal resistance 1 Ω is connected to a 9 Ω resistor. A resistor of resistance R is placed in parallel with the 9 Ω resistor, doubling the battery current. Find the numerical value of 5R when R is measured in Ω.
48. A screw gauge has pitch 0.5 mm and 50 circular-scale divisions. The main scale reads 5.0 mm and division 23 coincides with the reference line. Its positive zero error is 0.03 mm. Find the corrected reading in μm.
49. A 1 kg block moving at 6 m/s sticks to a stationary 2 kg block on a frictionless horizontal surface. The combined mass compresses an initially relaxed spring of force constant 300 N/m. Find the maximum compression in cm.
50. An open organ pipe and a pipe closed at one end have the same effective length. The open pipe’s fundamental frequency is 240 Hz. Find the closed pipe’s first overtone frequency in Hz.

## Chemistry

### Section A · Single-correct MCQs

51. A mixture contains 0.20 mol H₂ and 0.15 mol O₂. It is ignited and all water is removed after the reaction. How much gas remains?  
    A. 0.025 mol · B. 0.050 mol · C. 0.100 mol · D. 0.150 mol
52. A hydrogen atom in its ground state absorbs a photon of 12.75 eV. Take the ground-state energy as −13.60 eV. What is the maximum number of distinct emission lines across an ensemble of such excited atoms returning to the ground state?  
    A. 3 · B. 4 · C. 5 · D. 6
53. Which pair is correctly matched to the same molecular shape?  
    A. XeF₂ and CO₂ · B. SF₄ and CH₄ · C. ClF₃ and BF₃ · D. XeF₄ and NH₄⁺
54. Which order of first ionization enthalpy is correct?  
    A. B < C < N < O · B. C < B < O < N · C. B < C < O < N · D. B < O < C < N
55. For [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺, assuming a low-spin configuration, the number of unpaired electrons and the oxidation state of Co are  
    A. 4 and +3 · B. 0 and +3 · C. 1 and +2 · D. 3 and +3.
56. How many stereoisomers, counting enantiomers separately, are possible for [Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺, where en is ethane-1,2-diamine?  
    A. 1 · B. 2 · C. 3 · D. 4
57. Which pair illustrates lanthanoid contraction through very similar atomic radii?  
    A. Ti and Zr · B. Sc and Y · C. V and Nb · D. Zr and Hf
58. Which species has the greatest bond order according to molecular orbital theory?  
    A. O₂⁺ · B. O₂ · C. O₂⁻ · D. O₂²⁻
59. Given standard enthalpies of formation in kJ/mol of CO₂(g)=−394, H₂O(l)=−286 and CH₃OH(l)=−239, what is the standard enthalpy of combustion of liquid methanol?  
    A. −966 · B. −727 · C. −441 · D. −488
60. At 25 °C, aqueous acetic acid and sodium acetate have the same molar concentration in a buffer. If Kₐ=1.8×10⁻⁵, the pH is closest to  
    A. 4.26 · B. 4.74 · C. 5.26 · D. 9.26.
61. At the same temperature, which dilute aqueous solution has the greatest osmotic pressure? Assume complete dissociation where applicable.  
    A. 0.10 M glucose · B. 0.05 M NaCl · C. 0.04 M CaCl₂ · D. 0.025 M AlCl₃
62. A first-order reaction is 75% complete in 20 min. Starting from the same initial concentration, how long does it take to reach 87.5% completion?  
    A. 25 min · B. 40 min · C. 35 min · D. 30 min
63. For Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s), E°cell=1.10 V. At 298 K, [Zn²⁺]=1.0 M and [Cu²⁺]=0.010 M. Using concentrations for activities and 2.303RT/F=0.0592 V, find Ecell.  
    A. 1.041 V · B. 1.159 V · C. 0.982 V · D. 1.100 V
64. Which compound is the strongest acid?  
    A. Phenol · B. 4-Methylphenol · C. 4-Nitrophenol · D. Ethanol
65. How many distinct constitutional monochloro products result from replacing one H atom in 2,3-dimethylbutane by Cl?  
    A. 2 · B. 3 · C. 4 · D. 5
66. Reductive ozonolysis of 2-methylbut-2-ene yields  
    A. methanal and butan-2-one · B. two molecules of ethanal · C. propanone and methanal · D. propanone and ethanal.
67. Which compound reacts fastest by an Sₙ2 mechanism with iodide in a polar aprotic solvent?  
    A. (CH₃)₃CBr · B. CH₃Br · C. (CH₃)₂CHBr · D. CH₃CH₂Br
68. Which compound gives both a positive iodoform test and a positive Tollens’ test?  
    A. Propanone · B. Ethanol · C. Ethanal · D. Benzaldehyde
69. Benzenediazonium chloride reacts with CuCN. What is the principal organic product?  
    A. Benzonitrile · B. Benzyl cyanide · C. Phenyl isocyanide · D. Chlorobenzene
70. Which statement about sucrose is correct?  
    A. It contains a free anomeric carbon at each end. · B. Its hydrolysis gives two molecules of glucose. · C. It is a reducing disaccharide. · D. Its glycosidic bond involves both anomeric carbons.

### Section B · Numerical answers

71. Completely reduce MnO₄⁻ to Mn²⁺ with Fe²⁺ in acidic solution. Find the number of moles of Fe²⁺ required per mole of permanganate.
72. A solution of 5.85 g NaCl in 0.50 kg water freezes 0.670 °C below pure water. Use Kf=1.86 K·kg/mol and M(NaCl)=58.5 g/mol. If NaCl dissociates only into Na⁺ and Cl⁻, find the percentage degree of dissociation.
73. For a reaction with rate law r=k[A]²[B], [A] is tripled and [B] is reduced to one-third. Find the factor by which the rate changes.
74. Find the total number of stereoisomers of 2,3-dibromobutane, counting enantiomers separately.
75. In acidic medium, dichromate is reduced to Cr³⁺ as iodide is oxidized to I₂. How many moles of I₂ are produced per mole of dichromate?
